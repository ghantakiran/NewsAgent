NewsAgent — Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Introduction

NewsAgent ("we", "our", or "the App") is a stock market news aggregation application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

We collect the following data to provide and improve our services:

  • Email address — collected during account creation to identify your account and enable login.
  • Device token — collected to deliver push notifications to your device via Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  • Watchlist tickers — the stock ticker symbols you add to your watchlist, used to personalize your news feed and send relevant push notification alerts.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect personal financial data (portfolio holdings, account balances, etc.).
  • We do not collect trading data (orders, transactions, brokerage information).
  • We do not collect location data.

How We Use Your Data

  • Push notifications — we use your device token and watchlist tickers to send you timely alerts about news related to the stocks you follow.
  • Personalization — your watchlist tickers are used to prioritize and filter the news feed to show content most relevant to you.
  • Account management — your email address is used for authentication and account-related communications.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) — provided by Google, used to deliver push notifications. FCM may collect device identifiers as described in Firebase's Privacy Policy.

Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us. Upon receiving a deletion request, we will remove your data within 30 days.

Data Security

All data transmitted between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit using TLS. We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Children's Privacy

NewsAgent is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or by email. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
