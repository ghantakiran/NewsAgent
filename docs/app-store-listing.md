## Full Description (4000 chars max)

Stay ahead of the market with NewsAgent — your free, real-time stock market news aggregator built for active traders and investors who need information fast.

NewsAgent pulls from 19+ trusted financial news sources and delivers breaking headlines, analyst actions, earnings results, and market catalysts straight to your phone. No paywalls. No clutter. Just the news that moves markets.

**LIVE NEWS FEED**
Get a continuously updating stream of market-moving headlines from sources including Reuters, Bloomberg, MarketWatch, Seeking Alpha, Benzinga, and more. Our smart noise filter cuts through the clutter so you see what matters — not press releases about office lease renewals.

**ANALYST UPGRADES & DOWNGRADES**
Track analyst actions as they happen. Every upgrade, downgrade, and initiation is displayed in a clean 3-column layout showing the firm, action, and price target. Our proprietary grading system rates each analyst firm from A+ to C based on historical accuracy, so you know which calls to pay attention to.

**EARNINGS TRACKER**
Never miss an earnings report. See results as they drop with clear beat/miss indicators showing EPS and revenue vs. estimates. Quickly scan which companies crushed expectations and which disappointed — all in a single glance.

**CATALYST SCANNER**
Surface market-moving events across six categories: FDA decisions, M&A activity, IPO filings, insider transactions, dividend changes, and SEC filings. Filter by type to focus on the catalysts that matter to your strategy.

**PERSONAL WATCHLIST**
Build a watchlist of the tickers you care about. Get instant push notifications when news breaks on your stocks — analyst actions, earnings, catalysts, or breaking headlines. Never be the last to know.

**BY-SYMBOL GROUPING**
Tap any ticker badge to see all recent articles about that company in one place. Research a stock in seconds without scrolling through hundreds of unrelated headlines.

**CUSTOM RSS FEEDS**
Add your own RSS feeds to bring in niche sources, sector-specific newsletters, or proprietary research feeds. NewsAgent adapts to how you consume information.

**DESIGNED FOR TRADERS**
Built with a dark theme inspired by Bloomberg terminals, NewsAgent is optimized for extended monitoring sessions. The interface is fast, minimal, and information-dense — exactly how market professionals want it.
